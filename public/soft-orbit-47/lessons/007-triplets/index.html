Triplets: Three Inside One

Change the internal ruler from two equal pieces to three—without moving the beat.

⅓

A triplet divides one beat into three equal positions: 1-trip-let. Your foot still taps only the numbers. In 4/4, four beats produce 12 triplet positions—not 12 main beats.

How crowded is one beat?

Two parts · 1 &

Three parts · 1-trip-let

1triplet

Triplets are not 3/4. Three-four groups three main beats per bar. Triplet subdivision places three equal internal positions inside each beat, while the bar can remain 4/4.

The let after beat 2 occurs two-thirds of the way toward beat 3, later than the ordinary eighth-note &, which sits halfway.

Switch rulers without moving the floor

  1. Tap a steady quarter-note foot pulse.
  2. Say one bar of 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &.
  3. Then say 1-trip-let 2-trip-let….
  4. The numbers must arrive at identical moments in both bars.
