Sixteenths: Zoom Into the Beat

A finer ruler reveals the quick syllables hiding between familiar beats.

16

Sixteenth-note subdivision divides every beat into four equal positions: 1 e & a. Across 4/4, that creates 16 possible onset positions—but it does not require 16 notes.

Program a tiny rhythm

Tap cells to rewrite the hook. Thick-bordered cells are the main beats.

Example sixteenth-note hook

The e is one quarter of the way into a beat, & is halfway, and a is three quarters through—just before the next number. A syllable on the a of 2 sits on the last sixteenth grid line before beat 3.

Keep the hierarchy

Count 1 e & a 2 e & a… while your foot taps only numbers. The subdivisions are internal ruler marks; they do not replace the main pulse.
