Straight eighth notes divide a beat equally: the & sits at 50%. In swing, the number lasts longer and the & arrives later, creating a long–short relationship.

1& late2

A triplet grid gives a useful approximation: perform the first and third triplet positions—1 — LET 2 — LET—then rename each let as &. Real swing can be subtler or stronger than this exact ratio.

The numbers remain fixed. If beat 2 itself arrives later, you changed the tempo rather than swung the subdivision. In a DAW, swing controls shift selected offbeat notes while preserving the main beat grid.

Feel the hinge

  1. Tap numbers steadily.
  2. Clap straight eighths, exactly halfway.
  3. Recount triplets.
  4. Clap only number and let.
  5. Alternate straight and swung bars without moving your foot.
