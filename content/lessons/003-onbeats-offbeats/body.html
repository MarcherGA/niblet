Grounded: numbers

Propelled: ands

A note can begin offbeat and continue across a beat. Its onset stays offbeat even if most of its duration happens later. Beginning on the & of 4 before a new bar creates anticipation: the phrase reaches toward beat 1 early.

Two independent layers

Tap your foot on every number while clapping only on each &. If your foot starts following the claps, your reference has collapsed. The goal is to feel the stable ruler and the rhythm on top simultaneously.
