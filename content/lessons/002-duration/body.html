Every note has an onset, the instant it begins, and a duration, the time until it stops. A sound held through beats 1 and 2 is one two-beat note; restarting it on beat 2 creates two separate one-beat notes.

In 4/4, a whole note lasts four beats, a half note two, a quarter note one, and an eighth note half a beat. Rests use the same measured durations, but represent silence. Time continues through a rest.

On a piano roll, the left edge is the onset and the rectangle's width is duration. Empty grid space is a rest. Pitch lives vertically; rhythm lives horizontally.

Start–hold exercise

  1. Tap 1 2 3 4 continuously.
  2. Hum once on 1 and hold through 2; restart on 3 and hold through 4.
  3. Next, hum briefly on 1, stay silent on 2, then hum from 3 through 4.
  4. Keep tapping during both held notes and silence.
