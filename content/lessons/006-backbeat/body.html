Reference groove

A structurally strong beat and a loud sound are not identical. Beat 1 remains the beginning even if it is silent. A loud snare can make beats 2 and 4 prominent without turning either into the bar's beginning.

Body mixer

  1. Foot taps every number.
  2. Hands clap only 2 and 4.
  3. Voice enters on 2-& and holds through 3.

You are now performing pulse, backbeat, and syncopated melody as three independent layers.
