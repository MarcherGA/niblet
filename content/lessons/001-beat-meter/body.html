A beat is the pulse you can tap. Meter groups that continuing pulse into repeating shapes. In 4/4, the shape is ONE two three four; in 3/4, it is ONE two three. Three-four is not four-four with a missing beat—the next bar simply begins sooner.

Three-sided time

The top number of a time signature tells you how many beats form a bar. For now, the bottom 4 says the counted beat is represented by a quarter note. Tempo is separate: either meter can move slowly or quickly.

Bar lines are landmarks

Four bars of 4/4 contain 16 counted beats. Four bars of 3/4 contain 12. Changing meter moves the bar lines, but the pulse can stay at exactly the same speed.

Do it with your body

  1. Tap 16 evenly spaced pulses while counting four groups of four.
  2. Without changing speed, continue for 12 pulses as four groups of three.
  3. Make only each ONE slightly stronger.
  4. Notice that the accent—not a pause—tells you the shape.
